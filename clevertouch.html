Software Suite

The Clevertouch Software Ecosystem

Every Clevertouch board comes packed with powerful software tools designed to enhance teaching and collaboration.

LYNX Whiteboard

A powerful collaborative whiteboard application with thousands of ready-made lesson resources, templates and learning activities to save teachers time on lesson planning.

Cleverstore

A curated app store built specifically for education and business use. Download verified educational apps and productivity tools directly to your Clevertouch board.

Clevershare

A free wireless screen-sharing app compatible with Android, Windows, iOS and Chrome devices. Connect any device to your Clevertouch board instantly by entering the on-screen code.

ClevertouchLive

A central management platform that allows IT departments to update apps and firmware, and administrators to share content and instant messages across multiple devices from one place.
