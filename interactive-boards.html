Why Interactive Boards?

Transform the Way You Teach & Collaborate

Interactive boards are revolutionising education and business communication across the globe. By replacing traditional whiteboards and projector-screen setups, they create dynamic, immersive environments where ideas come to life through touch, gesture, and real-time digital interaction.

In the classroom, interactive boards dramatically increase student engagement and comprehension. Teachers can draw on limitless digital canvases, embed videos, run interactive quizzes, and access thousands of pre-built lesson resources — all from one device. Students become active participants rather than passive observers.

In the boardroom, interactive displays enable seamless collaboration between in-room and remote participants. Teams can annotate shared documents, wirelessly mirror any device to the screen, and capture every decision in real time — making meetings shorter, smarter, and more productive.

  • Enhanced Engagement & Participation
  • Multi-touch Collaboration
  • Wireless Screen Sharing
  • Built-in Software & Apps
