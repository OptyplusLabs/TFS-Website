Why Newline

What Makes Newline Stand Out

Newline interactive boards are built with a focus on simplicity, reliability and collaboration — giving users a seamless experience every time.

LYNX Whiteboard Software

Every Newline panel ships with LYNX, Newline's award-winning interactive whiteboard software. Draw, annotate, collaborate and share — all from one intuitive interface designed for educators and business professionals.

Wireless Screen Sharing

Participants can wirelessly share their screen from any device — laptop, tablet or phone — without cables or dongles. Supports Windows, macOS, iOS and Android for truly seamless collaboration.

Enterprise Security

Newline boards come with enterprise-grade security features including data encryption, remote management and multi-user authentication. Perfect for government organisations, banks and educational institutions.
