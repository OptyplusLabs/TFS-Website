What Are Interactive Kiosks?

Self-Service Technology for Modern Business

Interactive kiosks are touch-responsive self-service terminals that allow customers, students and visitors to access information, make payments and complete transactions independently — reducing queue times and improving service delivery.

The Future Store distributes a wide range of interactive kiosk and self-service payment terminal solutions across Zimbabwe and Southern Africa, serving retail, education, hospitality, healthcare and government sectors.

  • Touch-Responsive Interface
  • Self-Service Payment Processing
  • Information Dispensing & Wayfinding
  • Queue Management Integration
  • Remote Content Management
  • Vandal-Resistant Enclosures

Why Kiosks?

Benefits of Self-Service Kiosks

Reduce Operational Costs

Self-service kiosks handle routine transactions and enquiries automatically, freeing up staff for higher-value activities and reducing overhead costs.

Improve Customer Experience

Customers enjoy faster service with no queues. Kiosks are available 24/7 and provide consistent, accurate information at any time.

Increase Revenue

Digital kiosks can upsell products and services, display targeted promotions and process more transactions per hour than traditional counter service.

Collect Valuable Data

Gather insights on customer behaviour, peak usage times and popular services to make data-driven business decisions and improve operations.
